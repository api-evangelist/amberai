# Amber Public API

- [Introduction](https://docs.amber.ai/index.md): Read your complete Amber catalog and business records through one API
- [Authentication](https://docs.amber.ai/authentication.md): Get an API key, choose your brand, and handle rate limits
- [Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://docs.amber.ai/guides/mcp.md): Connect Claude or your own MCP client to read your Amber data
- [Find records by business identifiers](https://docs.amber.ai/tutorials/find-records.md): Find products, shipments, invoices, suppliers, and related records by the identifiers you already have
- [Export your product catalog](https://docs.amber.ai/tutorials/export-catalog.md): Read every Parent Product, Product Option, SKU, and option price for an ERP, PIM, or warehouse
- [Read product specifications](https://docs.amber.ai/tutorials/product-specifications.md): Assemble a Parent Product with every page of its included versions, BOMs, measurements, and options
- [Compare quotes and price breaks](https://docs.amber.ai/tutorials/compare-quotes.md): Follow a Quote through its visible revisions, items, and quantity bands without mixing up their IDs
- [Track orders and fulfillment](https://docs.amber.ai/tutorials/track-orders.md): Connect purchase order lines to shipments, received goods, and financial records
- [Download documents and images](https://docs.amber.ai/tutorials/download-documents.md): Find entity attachments, preserve shared-document access, and download files through temporary URLs
- [Filters by entity](https://docs.amber.ai/lookup-filters.md): Find records by code, reference, name, or related record
- [Product graphs](https://docs.amber.ai/product-graphs.md): Parent Products, Product Options, SKUs, and complete specification data
- [Entity relationships](https://docs.amber.ai/entity-relationships.md): Connect products, orders, shipments, payments, and their supporting records
- [Pagination and errors](https://docs.amber.ai/pagination.md): Retrieve every record, preserve precision, and retry transient failures
